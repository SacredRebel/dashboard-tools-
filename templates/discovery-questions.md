# Discovery questions

Ask only what the existing material (voice notes, transcripts, playbook) doesn't answer. In a live chat, ask one at a time. In person, use as a checklist.

## The business
1. In one sentence, what do you offer and to whom?
2. Who are your clients: individuals, businesses, or both? Roughly how many active at once?
3. Where do new clients come from? (referral, Instagram, website, events…)
4. What's a typical client worth, and how long from first contact to signed?

## The journey
5. Walk me through your last client from first message to finished. What were the steps?
6. Which step do people most often get stuck in or drop out of?
7. What has to be true before you move someone to the next step?
8. After the work is done, what happens? (referrals, repeat, check-ins)

## Today's tools & pains
9. Where do you track clients today? Can you show me or send a copy?
10. What falls through the cracks? (follow-ups, quotes, invoices, birthdays…)
11. What do you do every week that feels like admin you'd love to stop doing?

## The dashboard
12. When you open it in the morning, what do you want to see first?
13. What information do you need about each client? What do you *never* look at?
14. Which numbers tell you business is good or bad?
15. Phone, laptop, or both? Where are you when you use it?
16. Will anyone else use it (assistant, partner)? Should they see everything?

## Look & feel
17. Do you have a logo, brand colors, fonts, a website?
18. Show me 2–3 apps or websites you love the look of. What do you like about them?
19. Three words for how it should feel (e.g. calm, warm, luxurious, sharp, playful).
20. Anything you definitely don't want?

## Extras & ownership
21. Do you use Gmail / Google Calendar / Outlook? Should it connect?
22. Do you record meetings (Fathom, Zoom, Otter)? Would you like notes to land in the CRM?
23. Any sensitive data (health, finances, children)? Any privacy rules you follow?
24. Do you want to be able to change things yourself later with AI?
25. What would make you say "this is amazing" after one week of using it?
